Straight answers

What happens after I submit?
Standard (free): your track joins the review queue. A real human listens to every submission, in order — but we promise no timeline. The queue is the queue. Priority Spin ($10): you skip the line entirely. You get a guaranteed listen plus honest written feedback within 7 days, or your money back.
Do you guarantee placement?
No. Nobody can buy a feature, a playlist add, or a social post. The Priority Spin buys a guaranteed listen and honest feedback — never a yes. If a track is genuinely good enough, we'll consider it for a spotlight feature, a social post, or a city playlist. Consideration, not a promise.
What does the $10 actually get me?
A guaranteed human listen within 7 days and written feedback — even if the answer is no. It does not buy reach, exposure, or listener numbers. We're upfront: we don't have a big audience yet, and we won't sell you one. You're buying a real listen from people with taste.
How do you confirm my priority payment?
Keep your Stripe email receipt. During review we match the email on your submission against payments in our Stripe dashboard — a priority submission isn't reviewed until its payment is confirmed there.
