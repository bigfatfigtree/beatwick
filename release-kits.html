Straight answers

What exactly am I paying for?
The deliverables listed above — nothing else. Single Kit: 3 cover-art variants, 5 social graphics, a 150-word press blurb. EP Kit adds a PDF-ready EPK one-sheet, 15 social graphics total, and a release-week timeline with checklist. Delivered to your inbox within 5 business days of payment.
How does payment work?
Place your order here — nothing is charged on this form. We send a secure invoice to your email within 24 hours. Pay it and your 5-business-day turnaround starts. If our instant checkout is live, you'll go straight to secure payment instead.
Does this get me on playlists or press?
No. Release Kits are work product: artwork, graphics, copy. They don't include playlist placement, press outreach, streams, or any audience or reach guarantees. If anyone sells you those alongside design work, be skeptical — including us.
Who makes the artwork?
Our studio — every kit is directed by a human and built with professional design tools, then reviewed before delivery. You get one full revision round: tell us what's off and we rework it. Still not right after that? Full refund, no fight.
What if my release date is sooner than 5 business days?
Tell us in the order notes. If we can't hit your date, we'll say so before you pay — we'd rather turn down the order than miss your drop.
