LAGOS

The loudest A&R desk on the planet sits in traffic on the Lekki-Epe Expressway. Afrobeats didn't go global — it just waited for the world to catch up to Lagos, where Wizkid and Burna Boy turned a city's noise into the 21st century's default pop setting. Fela Kuti's Afrobeat was the blueprint; three generations later, Lagos still exports rhythm the way other cities export cars.

Lagos is Nigeria's commercial capital and the world capital of afrobeats — the modern pop sound built on Fela Kuti's Afrobeat blueprint, now the 21st century's default setting thanks to Wizkid and Burna Boy. The scene runs through Victoria Island's studios and the New Afrika Shrine in Ikeja, where the Kuti family keeps the annual Felabration festival burning.

Legends

Defined this city

  • Burna Boy

    The African Giant: grandson of Fela Kuti's manager Benson Idonije, 'Ye' (2018) turned pidgin philosophy into a continental anthem, and Twice as Tall took the 2021 Grammy for Best Global Music Album.

  • Wizkid

    A Surulere church-choir kid who became the smoothest voice in global pop — 'Ojuelegba' (2014), named for a Lagos neighborhood, went global, and 'One Dance' (2016) made him the first Nigerian artist to top the US Hot 100.

  • Asake

    Signed to Olamide's YBNL in 2022 and detonated immediately: Mr. Money With The Vibe fused fuji's streetwise melodies with amapiano's log drum.

Good to know

FAQ

What is the difference between Afrobeat and afrobeats?

Fela Kuti's Afrobeat — the 1970s big-band fusion of funk, jazz and Yoruba rhythms — was the blueprint. Modern afrobeats is the 21st-century pop sound Lagos built on top of it: Wizkid, Burna Boy, Davido and Rema turned it into the world's default party setting.

Where is the New Afrika Shrine?

In Ikeja, Lagos — run by the Kuti family as Fela's open-air temple and the spiritual home of Afrobeat. It hosts the annual Felabration festival in Fela's honor.

What is Felabration?

The annual festival at the New Afrika Shrine celebrating Fela Kuti's life and music — part concert, part pilgrimage, and the loudest week on Lagos's calendar.

Who are the biggest artists from Lagos right now?

Wizkid, Burna Boy, Davido, Asake, Rema, Ayra Starr and Tems sit at the top — with a street-pop wave (Seyi Vibez, Shallipopi, Ayo Maff, Muyeez) pushing up fast behind them.
