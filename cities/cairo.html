ON THE AIR · CAIRO

CAIRO

Mahraganat is the sound of Cairo's streets talking back — electronic shaabi born in working-class neighborhoods that the authorities tried to ban and the internet turned into the Arab world's biggest export. Wegz and Marwan Pablo turned rap into stadium currency; Hassan Shakosh and Omar Kamal turned a wedding song into a global phenomenon. Cairo doesn't follow genres. It absorbs them and spits them back louder.

FAQ

What is mahraganat?

Electronic shaabi born in Cairo's working-class neighborhoods — the Arab street sound the authorities tried to ban and the internet turned into the region's biggest export.

Where is El Sawy Culturewheel?

In Zamalek, on the Nile — the riverside arts engine that serves as the underground's unofficial HQ.

What is the Cairo Opera House?

The city's flagship cultural venue — grand halls where Egypt's classical and contemporary scenes collide.

Who are the biggest artists from Cairo right now?

Wegz and Marwan Pablo turned Egyptian rap into stadium currency — with Hassan Shakosh, Amr Diab and Cairokee holding the city's many other lanes.
