ON THE AIR — PARIS

PARIS

France is the biggest rap market on the planet outside America, and Paris is its engine room — from NTM's golden era to Booba's long reign to PNL's cloud-rap takeover. The banlieues built a sound the whole continent now copies, in a language the algorithm had to learn. The charts never really belonged to anyone else.

This is Beatwick's guide to what's moving in Paris in 2026 — 30 essential tracks, the historic rooms (L'Olympia, Le Zénith, Accor Arena), and the new voices breaking through.

Paris FAQ

What is the Paris music scene known for?

Two lanes running in parallel: French rap (Booba, PNL, Ninho, Gazo) that dominates the national charts, and French touch electro (Daft Punk, Justice, Kavinsky) that conquered the world. Aya Nakamura's pop sits at the center of it all.

Where do locals see live music in Paris?

L'Olympia in the 9th arrondissement for the career-defining night, Le Zénith at Parc de la Villette for the big rap shows, and the Accor Arena in Bercy for the stadium-grade homecoming.

Who are the new Paris artists to watch?

Our New to the Dial section tracks the next wave — boom-bap keeper Benjamin Epps and electro-pop prodigy Yoa — alongside spotlights on Daft Punk, Aya Nakamura and Booba.
