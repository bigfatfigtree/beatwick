STOCKHOLM

No city this size has any business writing this many of the world's biggest songs. From ABBA to the Cheiron hit factory to Max Martin — the most successful songwriter of the modern era — Stockholm treats pop like an industrial science, and the global charts are its lab report.

PHOTO: Julian Herzog (Website) · CC BY 4.0 VIA WIKIMEDIA COMMONS

Stockholm writes everyone's pop songs — the city behind ABBA, Max Martin and Avicii is still the planet's hit factory, with an indie underground that punches far above its weight. This is Beatwick's guide to what's moving in 2026 — 30 essential tracks, the key venues (Avicii Arena, Tele2 Arena, Debaser), and the new artists breaking through.

Stockholm FAQ

What is the Stockholm music scene known for?

Writing everyone's pop songs: ABBA, Max Martin, Avicii, Swedish House Mafia and an unbroken hit-factory lineage. Underneath, an indie underground — The Knife, Fever Ray, Viagra Boys, First Aid Kit — and a Swedish-language rap scene (Yasin, Dree Low, Einár) that dominates at home.

Where do locals see live music in Stockholm?

The Avicii Arena and Tele2 Arena in Johanneshov for the arena shows, and Debaser for the indie rite of passage.

Who are the new Stockholm artists to watch?

Our New to the Dial section tracks them — indie dramatist GRANT and Maja Francis. The names that defined the city (ABBA, Avicii and Robyn) live in the Legends list below.

The Beatwick: Stockholm Spotify playlist refreshes every week, and the Beatwick 30 chart below is updated weekly by our editors.
