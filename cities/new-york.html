On the air — New York

NEW YORK

Hip-hop was invented at a rec-room party on Sedgwick Avenue in 1973, and five decades later the five boroughs still out-produce every other city on earth per square mile. Bronx and Brooklyn drill (Pop Smoke's scene, Ice Spice's Bronx) runs the rap charts, the downtown indie lineage from CBGB to Bowery Ballroom never actually died, and the jazz rooms never closed. Density is the instrument — everything collides here first.

Good questions

FAQ

What is New York known for musically?

Hip-hop was born here — at a rec-room party on Sedgwick Avenue in the Bronx in 1973 — and the five boroughs still out-produce every other city per square mile. Today it's Bronx and Brooklyn drill running the rap charts, the downtown indie lineage from CBGB to Bowery Ballroom, and jazz rooms that never closed.

Who are the best new artists in New York?

Start with MIKE, Brooklyn's underground poet laureate running his own 10k label, and Water From Your Eyes, the Brooklyn duo making experimental dance music that keeps becoming everyone's favorite record. Ice Spice's Bronx drill is the commercial end of the same engine.

Where's the live music in New York?

Madison Square Garden in Midtown for coronations, the Apollo Theater in Harlem for proving-ground nights, and Radio City Music Hall for the Art Deco spectacle. The downtown indie circuit around the Bowery is still booking tomorrow's headliners.

Does New York still run rap?

Yes — Bronx and Brooklyn drill is the sound running the rap charts right now, from Pop Smoke's legacy to Ice Spice, A$AP Rocky and Cardi B. Density is the instrument: everything collides here first.
