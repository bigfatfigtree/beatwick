Good to know

FAQ

What is hiplife?

Ghana's homegrown sound — hip-hop fused with highlife — and the foundation Accra's current wave stands on. Sarkodie, Stonebwoy and M.anifest built the house; the new generation is knocking down the walls.

What is Detty December?

December in Accra — the continent's biggest party season, when the diaspora flies home and concerts stack up every night. The rest of the year is just rehearsal.

Where is Zen Garden?

In Labone, Accra — the city's live-music institution and the room where the new Ghanaian sound grows.

Who are the biggest artists from Accra right now?

Black Sherif, King Promise and Amaarae lead the new wave — with Sarkodie, Stonebwoy, KiDi, Gyakie and Camidoh holding the foundation.
