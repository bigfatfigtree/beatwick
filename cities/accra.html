ACCRA

Ghana's sound has always traveled further than its borders suggest, and this decade Accra is having its second coming. Hiplife built the house — Sarkodie, Stonebwoy, and Manifest laid the foundation — and now Black Sherif, King Promise, and Amaarae are knocking down the walls. December in Accra is the continent's biggest party; the rest of the year is just rehearsal.
