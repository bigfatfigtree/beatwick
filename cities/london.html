LONDON

Grime was born on pirate radio in Bow, drill mutated in Brixton stairwells, and now every label on earth keeps an A&R with a Zone 2 postcode. From Wiley's eskibeat to Stormzy shutting down the Brits to Dave and Central Cee's transatlantic link-ups, London doesn't follow global rap — it demos it first, then charges everyone else for the tutorial.
