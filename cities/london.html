LONDON

Grime was born on pirate radio in Bow, drill mutated in Brixton stairwells, and now every label on earth keeps an A&R with a Zone 2 postcode. From Wiley's eskibeat to Stormzy shutting down the Brits to Dave and Central Cee's transatlantic link-ups, London doesn't follow global rap — it demos it first, then charges everyone else for the tutorial.

London FAQ

What is the London music scene known for?

Grime, drill and UK rap — all prototyped in London, from Wiley's eskibeat in Bow to drill's mutation in Brixton. The city also gave the world Adele, Amy Winehouse and Stormzy shutting down the Brits.

Where do locals go to see live music in London?

The consensus rooms: Fabric in Farringdon for club nights, KOKO in Camden for the co-sign gig, and the Royal Albert Hall for the career-defining night. Brixton Academy's reopening was one of the decade's venue stories.

Who are the new London artists to watch?

Our New to the Dial section tracks them — including alté crooner Odeal and Catford genre-blender Jim Legxacy. The names that defined the city (Stormzy, Skepta and Adele) live in the Legends list below.
