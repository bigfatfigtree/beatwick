On the air — Atlanta

ATLANTA

Trap was invented in Atlanta's Zone 6 and East Point basements, and now it's the default language of global pop. OutKast proved the South had something to say in 1995; the conveyor belt since — Gucci Mane, Future, Migos, Young Thug — never stopped moving. The city exports producers the way Detroit exports cars, and every rap coronation still runs through downtown. Atlanta doesn't follow the culture. It manufactures it.

Good questions

FAQ

What is Atlanta known for musically?

Trap was invented in Zone 6 and East Point basements, and it's now the default language of global pop. OutKast proved the South had something to say in 1995; the conveyor belt since — Gucci Mane, Future, Migos, Young Thug — never stopped moving.

Who are the best new artists in Atlanta?

Anycia, the independent princess of trap who went viral on 'So What' and self-released Princess Pop That without signing a deal, and Slimesito, Clayton County's bilingual plugg ghost running the underground since the SoundCloud era.

Where's the live music in Atlanta?

State Farm Arena downtown — the endpoint of every rap coronation — the Fox Theatre in Midtown, still the prettiest room in the South, and the Tabernacle, a former downtown church hosting everything from rap to rock.

Why do producers matter so much in Atlanta?

The city exports producers the way Detroit exports cars — Metro Boomin, Zaytoven, Southside, and a hundred more. Every rap coronation still runs through downtown because the beats are made here first.
