GLASGOW

London gets the music press; Glasgow gets the bands. The Jesus and Mary Chain, Primal Scream, Teenage Fanclub, Mogwai, Belle and Sebastian, Franz Ferdinand — decade after decade, Scotland's biggest city produces guitar music the rest of the country wishes it made, usually while being completely ignored by the industry down south.
