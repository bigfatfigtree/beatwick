On the air — Toronto

TORONTO

Toronto spent decades as the stop between Chicago and Montreal, then Drake, The Weeknd, and Noah "40" Shebib built an entire sound out of late-night R&B murk — and the whole world copied it. The city runs on the 6ix's shadow now: Daniel Caesar's gospel-tinted soul, a deep indie lineage from Broken Social Scene, and the biggest Caribbean carnival outside the islands. The cold made the music stay inside and get weird.

Good questions

FAQ

What is Toronto known for musically?

Drake, The Weeknd and Noah "40" Shebib built an entire sound out of late-night R&B murk — and the whole world copied it. Underneath: Daniel Caesar's gospel-tinted soul, a deep indie lineage from Broken Social Scene, and the biggest Caribbean carnival outside the islands.

Who are the best new artists in Toronto?

River Tiber, the ghostwriter-in-plain-sight whose falsetto has been sampled by Drake and Kendrick, and LOONY, Scarborough's neo-soul secret with Polaris longlists and a Metro Boomin sample to her name.

Where's the live music in Toronto?

Scotiabank Arena downtown for the biggest tours, Massey Hall — the 130-year-old room every Canadian act has to headline — and Budweiser Stage, the lakeside amphitheater where summer actually feels like summer.

Why is Toronto called the 6ix?

It's Drake's nickname for the city — a nod to the 416 and 647 area codes — and it stuck so hard the whole city runs on its shadow now.
