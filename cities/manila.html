MANILA

P-pop is the Philippines' answer to K-pop — and it's winning on home turf. SB19 and BINI turned idol training into a national sport, while Ben&Ben and the OPM band tradition keep the guitar scene loud. Nobody does a live show like Manila: the crowds here sing every word, every time, at a volume that worries sound engineers.
