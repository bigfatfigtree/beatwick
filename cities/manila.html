MANILA

SB19 and BINI turned idol training into a national sport, while Ben&Ben and the OPM band tradition keep the guitar scene loud. GOOD QUESTIONS

FAQ

What defines Manila's music scene right now?

P-pop winning on home turf — SB19 and BINI turned idol training into a national sport — while Ben&Ben and the OPM band tradition keep the guitar scene loud.

What is P-pop?

Pinoy pop: idol groups trained like K-pop, singing in Filipino and English. SB19 built it from a Manila practice room; BINI took it to arenas.

Which Manila venues should I know?

SM Mall of Asia Arena in Bay City, the Cubao legend Araneta Coliseum, and New Frontier Theater — the mid-size room where the band scene lives.

Who are the new names on Manila's dial?

Munimuni, Quezon City's indie-folk poets of “makata pop,” and The Ridleys, named for Daisy Ridley on a dare and now headlining their own shows.
