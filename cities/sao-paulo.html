SÃO PAULO

South America's biggest city makes the continent's loudest party music. Baile funk's bruxaria — raw, favela-born, 150 BPM and climbing — went from Rio's bailes to São Paulo's pressure cooker and then global, with Anitta and MC Fioti carrying it past every border. Underneath the funk: the biggest electronic festival circuit in Latin America and a rock underground that never died. Paulistanos don't do quiet.
