Good questions

FAQ

What is New Orleans known for musically?

Jazz was born here, bounce was born here — Big Freedia still runs the bounce universe — and second-line culture turns every Sunday into a moving concert. Lil Wayne carried the city's rap flag worldwide while the brass bands kept the corners loud.

Who are the best new artists in New Orleans?

HaSizzle, the self-proclaimed King of Bounce whose voice is the city's secret weapon — sampled by Drake, storming Coachella — and Mia Borders, the independent soul queen with nine records on her own Blaxican Records.

Where's the live music in New Orleans?

Preservation Hall in the French Quarter, where traditional jazz never left the building; Tipitina's Uptown, the Neville Brothers' home turf and the bounce scene's big stage; and House of Blues for the big-tour circuit with gospel brunch on Sundays.

What is a second line?

New Orleans' real music industry: brass-band parades that turn Sundays into moving concerts, from funerals with full bands to neighborhood celebrations. The rest of the country rents its party culture from this tradition.
