NEW ORLEANS

The only American city where the funeral has a brass band and the parade never really ends. Jazz was born here, bounce was born here — Big Freedia still runs the bounce universe — and second-line culture turns every Sunday into a moving concert. Lil Wayne carried the city's rap flag worldwide while the brass bands kept the corners loud. The rest of the country rents its party culture from New Orleans; New Orleans never sent an invoice.
