ON THE AIR — BERLIN

BERLIN

In Berlin, techno isn't nightlife — it's civic infrastructure, protected like a monument, argued about like public transport, and taken as seriously as the rent debate. Berghain's door policy is folklore, Tresor is a bunker with a pulse, and producers from Paul Kalkbrenner to Ellen Allien turned abandoned power stations into the world's loudest town hall. UNESCO agreed in 2024, adding Berlin techno to its intangible cultural heritage list.

Berlin FAQ

What is the Berlin music scene known for?

Techno as civic infrastructure: Berghain's door folklore, Tresor's bunker pulse, and a producer lineage from Paul Kalkbrenner and Ellen Allien to Ben Klock. In 2024 UNESCO added Berlin techno to its intangible cultural heritage list. There's also a parallel German rap scene — Sido, K.I.Z, Capital Bra — that fills arenas.

Where do locals go clubbing in Berlin?

Berghain in Friedrichshain for the Sunday-morning service, Tresor in Mitte for thirty-plus years of 4/4, and Watergate in Kreuzberg for the riverside Panorama sound.

Who are the new Berlin producers to watch?

Our New to the Dial section covers the next wave — Ziúr's grindcore-club hybrids and Nene H's funky techno — plus spotlights on Ellen Allien, Moderat and Paul Kalkbrenner.
