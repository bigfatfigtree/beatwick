TOKYO

City pop died in 1989 and was resurrected by an algorithm — Tatsuro Yamashita and Mariya Takeuchi's glossy '80s funk became the internet's favorite time machine. Now Tokyo runs two sounds at once: the nostalgia economy and the feral present, where Creepy Nuts, King Gnu, and Vaundy turn J-rap and art-pop into chart monsters. Nobody remixes the past quite like this city.
