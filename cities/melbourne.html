MELBOURNE

Melbourne has more live music venues per capita than almost anywhere on Earth — this is the city that rallied to save The Tote, the little Collingwood bandroom that became a cause célèbre. It gave the world King Gizzard's infinite discography, Courtney Barnett's deadpan brilliance, and Amyl and the Sniffers' feral pub-punk. When people say Melbourne is Australia's cultural capital, what they mean is: the gigs.

GOOD QUESTIONS

FAQ

What defines Melbourne's music scene right now?

More live venues per capita than almost anywhere on Earth. The city rallied to save The Tote; it gave the world King Gizzard's infinite discography, Courtney Barnett's deadpan brilliance, and Amyl and the Sniffers' feral pub-punk.

What is a band room?

Melbourne's small, sticky-floored live rooms — the real music industry here. Our venue-culture dispatch explains why the band room matters more than the boardroom.

Which Melbourne venues should I know?

Forum Theatre on Flinders Street, the Sidney Myer Music Bowl for summer nights, and The Tote in Collingwood — the punk institution the city saved.

Who are the new names on Melbourne's dial?

Acopia, Naarm's dream-pop trio with three albums in three years, and Sleep D, the DIY duo behind Butter Sessions.
