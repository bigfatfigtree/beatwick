MELBOURNE

Melbourne has more live music venues per capita than almost anywhere on Earth — this is the city that rallied to save The Tote, the little Collingwood bandroom that became a cause célèbre. It gave the world King Gizzard's infinite discography, Courtney Barnett's deadpan brilliance, and Amyl and the Sniffers' feral pub-punk. When people say Melbourne is Australia's cultural capital, what they mean is: the gigs.
