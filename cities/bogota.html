BOGOTÁ

Latin alternative's laboratory sits at 2,600 meters, and the altitude does something to the music. Bomba Estéreo took cumbia into the global festival circuit from a Bogotá base, Aterciopelados and Monsieur Periné built the city's alt-pop identity, and Rock al Parque remains the biggest free rock festival in Latin America. Medellín's reggaetón gets the headlines; Bogotá does the experiments — tropical futurism with a mountain chill.
