On the air — Nashville

NASHVILLE

Nashville runs on a songwriting economy no other city has: publishing houses on Music Row, co-writes as daily labor, and a Lower Broadway honky-tonk strip that never closes. The country machine — Dolly, Cash, a century of the Opry — shares the zip code with Jack White's Third Man Records and a rap underground that's done waiting its turn. Every song you hum was probably written within ten blocks of here.

Good questions

FAQ

What is Nashville known for musically?

A songwriting economy no other city has: publishing houses on Music Row, co-writes as daily labor, and a Lower Broadway honky-tonk strip that never closes — plus a century of the Grand Ole Opry, broadcasting Saturday nights since 1925.

Who are the best new artists in Nashville?

Madi Diaz, the indie-folk survivor who took 14 years and an ANTI- deal to get her due, and Becca Mancari, East Nashville's genre escape artist who went from Americana darling to polyrhythmic pop.

Where's the live music in Nashville?

The Grand Ole Opry in Opryland — the church of country music — the Ryman Auditorium downtown, the Mother Church with the best-sounding room in America, and the Bluebird Cafe in Green Hills, the 90-seat listening room where songs get decided.

Is Nashville just country?

No. The country machine shares the zip code with Jack White's Third Man Records, a rap underground that's done waiting its turn, and rock bands like Paramore and Kings of Leon who call it home.
