JAKARTA

Dangdut koplo is Indonesia's biggest musical export that the world hasn't noticed yet — a hyperkinetic folk-pop hybrid from Java that runs on YouTube views instead of radio. Via Vallen and Nella Kharisma built empires on wedding-party energy; Denny Caknan and Happy Asmara turned heartbreak into stadium singalongs. It's the sound of 270 million people dancing in a traffic jam.
