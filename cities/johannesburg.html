JOHANNESBURG

Joburg invented a genre from a bassline that sounds like a log drum being dropped down stairs, then watched the whole planet dance to it. Amapiano grew out of township house parties into the most exported South African sound since kwaito — Kabza De Small and DJ Maphorisa turned bedroom production into a global industry. Nobody does a groove like this city; everyone else is just remixing.
