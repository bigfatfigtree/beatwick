ON THE AIR — AMSTERDAM

AMSTERDAM

Every October the entire electronic music industry descends on Amsterdam for ADE, and the rest of the year the city just gets on with being the genre's unofficial capital. Tiësto, Armin van Buuren, Martin Garrix, Afrojack — the Dutch didn't just dominate the mainstage, they practically invented the modern DJ as a stadium act.

PHOTO: Basile Morin · CC BY-SA 4.0 VIA WIKIMEDIA COMMONS

Amsterdam is dance music's R&D lab and Dutch hip-hop's hometown — the city that gave the world Martin Garrix, Tiësto and Afrojack also runs a homegrown rap scene in Dutch. This is Beatwick's guide to what's moving in 2026 — 30 essential tracks, the legendary venues (Paradiso, Melkweg, Ziggo Dome), and the new artists to watch.

Amsterdam FAQ

What is the Amsterdam music scene known for?

Dance music's R&D lab: Martin Garrix, Tiësto, Afrojack, Hardwell, Armin van Buuren and a conveyor belt of festival headliners all came up here. Running parallel is Dutch-language hip-hop — Lil' Kleine, Ronnie Flex, Boef — that sells out the Ziggo Dome.

Where do locals see live music in Amsterdam?

Paradiso in De Pijp and Melkweg on Leidseplein for the rite-of-passage gig, and the Ziggo Dome in Amsterdam-Zuidoost for the arena show. ADE (Amsterdam Dance Event) takes over the whole city every October.

Who are the new Amsterdam artists to watch?

Our New to the Dial section tracks them — IDM naturalist upsammy and house obsessive Tracey — plus spotlights on Martin Garrix, Tiësto and Afrojack.
