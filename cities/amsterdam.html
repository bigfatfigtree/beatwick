AMSTERDAM

Every October the entire electronic music industry descends on Amsterdam for ADE, and the rest of the year the city just gets on with being the genre's unofficial capital. Tiësto, Armin van Buuren, Martin Garrix, Afrojack — the Dutch didn't just dominate the mainstage, they practically invented the modern DJ as a stadium act.
