ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · NEW ORLEANS

HASIZZLE

The self-proclaimed King of Bounce — his voice is New Orleans' secret weapon, sampled by Drake, storming Coachella, and still running the city.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · AUGUST 26, 2026 · 3 MIN READ

Listen → Listen on Spotify

Start with “Bounce It Biggity Bounce It” — the first-ever HaSizzle production — official audio.

Hasan "HaSizzle" Matthews calls himself the King of Bounce, and New Orleans — a city that does not hand out royal titles lightly — mostly agrees. Born in the Calliope projects and the last bounce artist to break before Hurricane Katrina in 2005, HaSizzle has spent two decades building a catalog of nonstop party music with one of the most recognizable voices in the genre: high-energy, call-and-response, tongue trills, pure joy as a mandate. His tunes are Superdome playlist mainstays during Saints games. His ad-libs have been sampled by Drake, City Girls, Megan Thee Stallion, Sexyy Red, and Lil Wayne. He's a local institution who keeps showing up on the world's biggest stages.

The resume reads like a fever dream. Drake sampled "She Rode That Like a Soldier" on "Child's Play" from 2016's VIEWS. In 2023, HaSizzle turned up in Frank Ocean's much-anticipated Coachella closing set — disguised as a security guard, twerking through an extended on-camera spot that became one of the festival's defining images. Classics like "Bounce It Biggity Bounce It," "Hottie Tottie" with Sissy Nobby, and "Getcha Sum" with Anjelika "Jelly" Joseph built the crown; the 2025 project Bra$$ and A$$ — his self-described "gumbo" fusing bounce with live jazz, brass, blues, and hip-hop, featuring Mia X, Kermit Ruffins, and Pell — is the victory lap, with "Somebody Roll the Weed" as the smoke anthem.

WHY NEW ORLEANS CLAIMS HIM

Because bounce is New Orleans' own invention and HaSizzle is its most tireless ambassador. He came up in the post-Katrina scene, helped carry the sound from the projects to the global stage without diluting a drop of it, and — in a 2025 interview — said his goal is taking bounce to the Grammys. Twenty years in, the voice hasn't lost a step, the parties haven't stopped, and the city still treats every HaSizzle set like a home game.

THE KEY TRACK

"Bounce It Biggity Bounce It" — the one that launched him into the next realm, and still the purest distillation of the HaSizzle formula: nonstop energy, call-and-response, and a beat that refuses to let you stand still. For the proof of the longevity, "Somebody Roll the Weed" from Bra$$ and A$$ brings the full brass band.

THE VOICE

The instrument is the voice itself — a one-man party, ad-libs stacked like percussion. When Drake, the Saints, and the Grammys-aspiring bounce scene all need the same voice, it's not a voice anymore. It's a civic resource.

START HERE

Bra$$ and A$$ for the full-gumbo current version, then "Bounce It Biggity Bounce It" and "Getcha Sum" for the classics, then The Voice series for the raw live energy.

HaSizzle didn't wait for the world to find bounce. He carried it there himself, one party at a time.
