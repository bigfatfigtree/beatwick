ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · MELBOURNE

SLEEP D

The DIY duo behind Butter Sessions — pillars of Melbourne's underground who turned bush doofs and chamber orchestras into one rave.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · OCTOBER 5, 2026 · 3 MIN READ

Listen → Listen on Spotify

No official “Central” video could be verified — start with the record.

Maryos Syawish and Corey Kikos are childhood friends, of Iraqi and Maltese lineage, who grew up to become the two-man assembly line running one of Australia's most important electronic labels. As Sleep D, they're sonic explorers of what their bio calls "the rave unconscious" — driving rhythms, hypnotic bass, worlds of echo, the hazy bliss of the morning after. As the heads of Butter Sessions, they're pillars of the Melbourne/Naarm underground who ushered in a new era of productivity for the city that raised them. Two jobs, one frequency.

The records tell the story. Debut LP Rebel Force (2019) landed on Incienso — the imprint run by Anthony Naples and Jenny Slattery — a ten-track trip through mutated house, bush doof, dub techno, and downtempo that DUBIKS called rated "E" for Everyone. 2021's Flashed Glass paired them with the Ad Lib Collective chamber ensemble, merging club euphoria with concert-hall space — a record literally born in a Melbourne underground carpark and finished during the city's lockdowns with DIY home-recorded samples of crushed plastic and pulverized sea shells. Then 2023's Electronic Arts, and 2025's Big Sky, Liquid Sun EP on their own Butter Sessions. They even rescored Eraserhead and Pan's Labyrinth for live performance. Range is not the question.

Their live show has taken them from house parties on Melbourne's outskirts to Boiler Room and Sugar Mountain, across the US, UK, Europe, and Asia. And Butter Sessions — now fifteen years deep — remains a defiantly DIY operation releasing a steady stream of diverse records from the duo and their international kin on Incienso, Planet Euphorique, and Cocktail d'Amore. The community is the catalog.

WHY MELBOURNE CLAIMS THEM

Because Sleep D are Melbourne's underground made flesh — the city's most beloved DJ and production duo, with long-standing residencies and an ever-evolving live show fit for clubs and vast paddocks alike. If you've danced in Naarm in the last decade, you've probably danced to them without knowing it. Now you know.

THE KEY TRACK

"Central" (2019). The Rebel Force centerpiece — deep desert chug that builds into the best rave in town. It's the track that makes the whole "bush doof meets mutated house" thesis click in one hypnotic groove.

THE SOUND

Driving rhythms and hypnotic bass with worlds of echo: deep house to peak-time techno, dub techno shuffles, acid scraps, and ambient drift. "Green Thumbs" is electronica at 85 BPM; "Mountain Ash" is deep house at 139. The tempo changes; the trance doesn't.

START HERE

"Central," then Rebel Force front to back, then Big Sky, Liquid Sun for the current state of the union.

Sleep D don't just play Melbourne's underground — they run the label, book the ethos, and export the whole beautiful noise.
