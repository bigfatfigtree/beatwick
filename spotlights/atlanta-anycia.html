ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · ATLANTA

ANYCIA

Atlanta's independent princess of trap — viral on 'So What', self-released Princess Pop That, and never signed a deal.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · JUNE 3, 2026 · 3 MIN READ

Listen → Listen on Spotify

Start with “So What” — the viral 2023 breakout — the official music video.

Anycia Symone Edwards started rapping professionally in 2022, went viral off a snippet of "So What" in 2023, and by 2024 had a debut album, a Latto feature, a Veeze tour, and a Kehlani tour slot — all while staying independent and distributing through UnitedMasters. In a city where the trap giants vacuum up every buzzing newcomer, Anycia built a lane as Atlanta's underground princess on her own terms: a husky, laconic flow, a playful pen, and beats that nod to the city's 2000s heyday without living in the past. The AJC called it laid-back confidence with braggadocious flair. Rolling Stone called the voice sultry and smoky. The underground just calls her the real thing.

The run is relentless. The 2023 EP Extra introduced the nonchalance; the April 2024 debut Princess Pop That — self-released, preceded by the single "Bad Weather" — buttoned it up without sanding it down (Pitchfork's words). "Back Outside" with Latto went wide. Then came the hometown victory lap: Grady Baby, a DJ Drama-hosted Gangsta Grillz mixtape named for Grady Memorial Hospital, built on Lil Jon and Waka Flocka samples and old-Atlanta family-photo aesthetics. The 2025 single "Never Need" with GloRilla and Karrahbooo did millions of views in weeks. Independent, owns her publishing, and — per the reviews — just getting started.

WHY ATLANTA CLAIMS HER

She's pure city: the alter ego Princess Pop That, the Grady Hospital mythology, the Latto Birthday Bash stages, the lineage from Crime Mob to JetsonMade. While the trap giants operate on a different floor entirely, Anycia is the one the actual Atlanta underground argues about, reposts, and shows up for. Her independence isn't a talking point — it's the business model that lets her move at underground speed.

THE KEY TRACK

"So What" — the 2023 viral breakout, melodic plugg with Auto-Tune doing exactly what it's supposed to do. It's the song that turned a snippet into a career. For the current form, "Never Need" shows what happens when that casual flow meets GloRilla's intensity and holds its own.

THE FLOW

Uproxx compared her conversational nonchalance to Veeze; Billboard clocked the raspy, blasé tone. The bars are slower, casual, funny — pop-leaning beats with wit that sneaks up. It's trap that winks.

START HERE

Princess Pop That first, then Grady Baby for the hometown thesis, then the Extra EP for the raw early version. "BREAK" and "No Scrub" for the 2025 momentum.

Anycia is Atlanta's underground proving you don't need a deal — you need a sound, a city, and the nerve to own both.
