ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · LAGOS

ASAKE

Mr. Money fused Lagos street fuji with amapiano's log drum and became the fastest-rising star of the decade.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · MAY 14, 2026 · 3 MIN READ

Nobody has ever risen this fast. Ahmed Ololade — Asake, Mr. Money — signed to Olamide's YBNL in 2022 and immediately detonated. His debut, Mr. Money With The Vibe (2022), was one of the biggest Nigerian streaming debuts ever recorded, and he hasn't slowed down since.

The sound is the story: fuji's streetwise melodies fed through amapiano's log drum, Yoruba street slang over production that borrows from everywhere and belongs to Lagos. “Organise” (2022) announced him. “Lonely at the Top” (2023) made him unavoidable. Work of Art (2023) and Lungu Boy (2024) — the latter carrying “MMS” with Wizkid, which earned a Grammy nomination — confirmed the machine has no off switch.

WHY LAGOS CLAIMS HIM

Lagos-raised, Lagos-made. The Mr. Money sound is the city's street fuji tradition run through a 2020s club system — the danfo buses and the Detty December stages in one package. When Asake headlines December in Lagos now, it's a coronation the whole city attends.

THE KEY TRACK

“Lonely at the Top” (2023). The victory lap as mission statement — Asake surveying everything he built in eighteen months and sounding completely certain about the next eighteen.

THE SOUND

The formula deserves a closer listen: fuji — the Yoruba street-party music pioneered by giants like Wasiu Ayinde — fed through amapiano's log drum and modern Afrobeats polish, mostly produced by Magicsticks. It's ancestral and futuristic at once, the sound of Lagos's past and present arguing happily in the club.

THE VELOCITY

Three albums in three years, a Grammy nomination for “MMS” with Wizkid, and arena tours while most artists are still working on their debut rollout. Asake's velocity broke the industry's understanding of how fast a career can move — and forced every label in Lagos to rewrite their timelines.

THE TOUR

The live show is the other half of the phenomenon — Asake’s arena sets are precision-engineered chaos, log drums at maximum volume and a crowd that knows every word in three languages. Lagos exports a lot of things; right now its loudest export is holding a microphone.

START HERE

“Lonely at the Top,” then “Organise” for the origin, then “MMS” to hear two Lagos generations on one track.

Lagos doesn't do slow rises. Asake is just the fastest version of the city's oldest story.
