ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · MANILA

THE RIDLEYS

Named for Daisy Ridley on a dare, raised on UST battle-of-the-bands stages — Manila's most earnest indie-folk band just headlined their own show.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · AUGUST 28, 2026 · 3 MIN READ

Listen → Listen on Spotify

Start with “Be With You” — a live Cozy Cove performance.

The best band names are accidents, and The Ridleys' is perfect: in 2016, architecture student Bryant Ayes dragged three fellow musicians — vocalist Benny Manaligod, guitarist Jan De Vera, and bassist Joric Canlas — into the University of Santo Tomas Architecture Week Battle of the Bands. They needed a name fast. Manaligod had a crush on Daisy Ridley. The deadline loomed. The Ridleys it was. They didn't win the contest. A decade later, the name is synonymous with Manila's indie-folk scene — which tells you everything about how little winning matters when the songs are good.

Signed to the independent label Underdog Music PH, The Ridleys have been prolific in the quiet, steady way that builds real careers: the Aphrodite EP (2016), debut album Reflections on Moonlight and Poetry (2019), the two-volume Until I Reach the Sun EPs (2022–2023), and the 2024 sophomore album All These and More with its standout "Be With You." Nearly forty songs across streaming platforms, six releases deep — the kind of catalog that exists because the band kept showing up to open mics and small university stages while juggling school, day jobs, and the terrifying math of going full-time.

The milestone moments are stacking up. In November 2024 they headlined their first solo concert, "Someday We'll Make a Home," two sold-out nights at the Music Museum tracing their eight-year journey. They've opened for Kodaline's Manila show, played the Thomasian Welcome Party as returning heroes, and joined Morissette's 15th-anniversary Ember concert at the Smart Araneta Coliseum in October 2025. In 2026 they added bassist LJ Panganiban and announced their tenth anniversary. The crush-named band grew up.

WHY MANILA CLAIMS THEM

Because The Ridleys are Manila's great indie-folk underdogs — the band that tells stories through songs about friendship, faith, and growing up, in a scene that rewards exactly that earnestness. They play bars, schools, and stadiums with the same intensity.

THE KEY TRACK

"Be With You" (2024). The centerpiece of All These and More — harmonized, heartfelt, and built for the kind of singalong that made their Music Museum shows feel like family reunions. It's the band's thesis: sincerity as a strategy.

THE SOUND

Indie-pop and alternative folk with pristine harmonies and narrative songwriting: Manaligod's warm vocals up front, acoustic guitars and swelling arrangements behind. "Aphrodite," "Meaningful Silence (Our Song)," the Over October collaboration "Intertwine" — each one a carefully crafted short story with a chorus.

START HERE

"Be With You," then "Aphrodite" for the origin, then Until I Reach the Sun — the deeply personal fifteen-track record that was CD-and-Bandcamp-only until its 2024 digital release.

The Ridleys didn't win the battle of the bands. They won the decade.
