ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · SÃO PAULO

MC LUANNA

From São Paulo's baile circuit to shared bills with rap legends — MC Luanna makes lean, lethal funk that blew up track by track on her own terms.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · DECEMBER 3, 2025 · 3 MIN READ

No major-label rollout, no playlist bribery — MC Luanna's music spread the old-fashioned way: track by track, baile by baile, until São Paulo couldn't pretend she wasn't there. Her catalogue — "44," "Esconde Sua Namorada," "+18," "Trabalhosa" — is a masterclass in doing more with less: hard beats, sharper tongue, zero fat.

She reps the new wave of funk feminino, where the MCs run their own economies. Her TikTok audience runs into the hundreds of thousands, built single by single rather than bought in bulk. And she's not just a screen artist: in 2023 she shared a Sesc bill with veteran MCs Coruja BC1 and Dexter, proof the older generation already reads her name with respect.

WHY SÃO PAULO CLAIMS HER

São Paulo is where funk got its hardest edge, and Luanna is pure product of that pressure. The city rewards MCs who can hold a room with nothing but a flow and an attitude, and her music sounds like it was engineered for exactly that test — compact, confrontational, impossible to half-listen to.

THE KEY TRACK

"44." It's the kind of record that explains her whole appeal in two minutes: a beat with no mercy, a vocal that refuses to blink, and a title that does half the talking. Her most-played for a reason.

THE HUSTLE

The "Irrefreável" era — unstoppable is the word she picked for herself, and it's accurate. Luanna treats every release like a live round: no bloated albums, no filler, just a steady stream of singles built to travel. Independent, direct, and allergic to waiting around for someone else's gate to open.

She's also riding the wider funk feminino wave that's finally getting its due — the new generation of women MCs who took the baile sound their male counterparts built and sharpened it. Luanna's edge is her economy: no major-label machine, no bloated rollout, just a TikTok audience built one clip at a time and a catalogue where every track has a job. Her listeners treat her like a peer, not a product — which is exactly why the numbers keep compounding.

START HERE

"44," then "Esconde Sua Namorada," then "Trabalhosa." Three tracks and you'll understand exactly why the bailes keep booking her.

MC Luanna didn't wait for São Paulo's funk scene to notice her. She made it impossible not to.
