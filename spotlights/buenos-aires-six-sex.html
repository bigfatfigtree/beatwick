ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · BUENOS AIRES

SIX SEX

The Buenos Aires club queen who invented ravetón — reggaetón dragged through the rave — went from SoundCloud experiments to Remezcla's #1 dance song of 2024 and a debut album called ULTRA.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · MAY 6, 2026 · 3 MIN READ

Listen → Listen on Spotify

No official “4 noviosS” video could be verified — start with the record.

Six Sex makes a genre of her own and then dares you to keep up. Born Francisca Cuello in Villa Tesei, Buenos Aires Province, she calls it ravetón — reggaetón, dancehall, and techno smashed together at high BPM with a dark, sensual visual identity. She started in 2019 with the independently released Fantasy EP, and by 2026 she had Ultra, her debut album, with a trail of EPs — Área 69, 6X, Satisfire, X-Sex — behind her like scorched earth.

The turning point was "Duro" in 2021, the single that pulled her from underground party audiences to Lollapalooza, Cosquín, and Primavera Sound. Then "4 noviosS" became Remezcla's #1 dance song of 2024 — an anthem of high-BPM intensity that put the whole alt-urbano wave on notice. Rolling Stone and NME both put her on artists-to-watch lists. The scene's elders noticed too: she took a slot at the Buenos Aires Trap festival alongside Duki and Cazzu.

WHY BUENOS AIRES CLAIMS HER

Because she came up through its underground the hard way — independent releases, rave rooms, and perreo parties — and never left that energy behind when the big stages called. Her dance background bleeds into every performance: choreography, fashion, and theatrical presentation aren't extras, they're the music. She's a Buenos Aires product who can move between Latin perreo culture and international club circuits without belonging to either.

THE KEY TRACK

"4 noviosS." Remezcla's top dance song of 2024 for a reason — it's the purest dose of ravetón, high-BPM perreo with rave teeth. Start there, then "Duro" for the turning point, then anything with Dillom on it.

THE COLLABORATIONS

Her radar is genuinely international without losing the thread: tracks with LSDXOXO, the X-Sex EP's Dillom link-up, and sessions stretching from Mexico to Miami. Every collaboration bends toward her sound, never the other way around.

The evolution is visible release by release. Fantasy in 2019 was rawer and more electronic; Área 69 in 2022 sharpened the voice; the "REBOTA" and "UNA GATA" singles of 2023 and the 6X EP pushed the perreo harder; then Satisfire consolidated it all into something critics and dancefloors agreed on. Every era has been louder than the last, and Ultra — her first full-length — is the sound of an artist who's finally playing at the scale her ambition always demanded.

START HERE

"4 noviosS," then Satisfire, then Ultra front to back. Wear something you can dance in.

Six Sex didn't join Argentina's perreo scene. She built a faster one around it.
