ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · LAGOS

MUYEEZ

A teenager from Lagos who freestyled his way into Seyi Vibez's camp, topped the Nigerian charts on his debut single, and became street-pop's most convincing child prodigy.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · DECEMBER 24, 2025 · 3 MIN READ

Listen → Listen on Spotify

Start with “Instagram” — the 2024 debut that hit number one — the official video.

Moshood Abdulmuiz, known as Muyeez, started the way most of Lagos' street-pop class starts now: unpolished freestyle videos shot on a phone, a raw voice doing all the production. The difference is what happened next. Seyi Vibez signed the kid to Vibez Inc, and the debut single, "Instagram" (April 2024), shot straight to number one on Apple Music Nigeria and past 43 million Spotify streams. At an age when most people are revising for exams, Muyeez became one of the youngest working musicians in Nigeria — and the clearest sign that the street-pop conveyor belt now runs on teenagers.

His debut EP, Muyeez (2024), doubled down on the formula: rapid-fire, singsong melodies in Yoruba and pidgin over lightweight Afrobeats rhythms, four tracks, zero fat. The deluxe edition and the Hat-Trick EP (2024) arrived the same year — the latter bringing in label boss Seyi Vibez, Reekado Banks, Shallipopi, and Tml Vibez, a roll call that reads like a coronation. Then came "Mortal Kombat" (2025), proof he isn't standing still. In Lagos, where every bus stop has an opinion about the future of Afrobeats, Muyeez is the name that ends arguments.

WHY LAGOS CLAIMS HIM

Everything about him is the city's current music industry in miniature: discovered on social media, signed by a Lagos street-pop star, built for the charts on day one. The rapid singsong delivery and the Yoruba-pidgin code-switching are pure Lagos DNA — this is music for the danfo queue and the late-night ride home.

THE KEY TRACK

"Instagram" (2024), the debut that hit number one. It has everything the Muyeez blueprint promises: the breathless melodic runs, the street confidence of someone twice his age, and a chorus that Lagos hummed for months. When the co-sign from Seyi Vibez lands mid-song, it feels less like a feature and more like a torch being passed.

THE VOICE

The voice is the whole instrument: young, nasal, insistent, moving in tight melodic sprints that rarely stop to breathe. He doesn't have a singer's polish yet — that's the point. There's an unvarnished quality to the freestyles that survived the studio transition, which is why the kids who found him on social media still recognize him on the EPs.

START HERE

"Instagram," then the Muyeez EP front to back, then Hat-Trick for the heavyweight features. Finish with "Mortal Kombat" to see where 2025 is taking him.

Muyeez didn't wait for the industry's permission. In Lagos street-pop, teenagers don't ask.
