ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · BERLIN

NENE H

Berlin-via-Istanbul funky techno with a heart — the UMAY founder whose grief record Ali became one of the underground's most honest albums.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · JANUARY 21, 2026 · 3 MIN READ

Listen → Listen on Spotify

No official “Fukken Lie” video could be verified — start with the record.

Resident Advisor called it “extremely funky techno from Berlin via Istanbul,” and that barely scratches it. Nene H — Istanbul-born, Berlin-based — makes techno that collides with electro, ghettotech, trance and old-school bounce, all played with a grin. Her 2022 Trifecta EP on Live From Earth Klub is three tracks for the three cities that made her: Istanbul, Berlin, Copenhagen, complete with a voice memo announcing a canceled bedtime. Rattling kick drums, jabbing vocal samples, and enough groove to embarrass DJs with twice her profile.

Then she made Ali علي (2021) on New York's Incienso — a grief record. Her father died, and she spent two years making and remaking tracks that process it, caught between Berlin and Turkey, between the person with tattoos and the person who becomes her younger self back home. It's the rare techno album that's about something, and it made her an RA podcast pick, a Dazed mix subject, and a festival name that keeps climbing the bill.

WHY BERLIN CLAIMS HER

She runs UMAY, her own imprint spotlighting SWANA, Muslim and femme perspectives in club music, and she's a fixture of the queer underground circuit — Possession nights, Intrepid Skin releases, Berghain invitations. Berlin's techno scene talks a lot about funk; Nene H is one of the few actually bringing it.

THE KEY TRACK

“Fukken Lie” (2022). The centerpiece of Trifecta: buoyant, blistering, '90s house and techno energy bent through her severe personal style. The title track of her dancefloor mode — everything on UMAY in miniature.

THE VOICE

Not a vocalist — an arranger's voice, audible in how the samples land. Jabbing, funny, occasionally filthy, always physical. Her sets flit between techno, electro and ghettotech the way most DJs change shirts, and her productions carry the same restlessness. The duality she talked about around Ali — two cities, two selves — is the engine of everything.

START HERE

Trifecta for the party, Ali for the ache, then Second Skin and the Seltsame Fabeln EP (2025) for where she's going. Then watch an RA mix and understand why promoters in Tbilisi and Copenhagen keep booking her back.

Nene H makes techno like she means it — funny, furious, and completely uninterested in sounding like anyone else.
