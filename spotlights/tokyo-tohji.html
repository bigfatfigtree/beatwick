ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · TOKYO

TOHJI

Tokyo's most elusive rap figure: the Mall Boyz MC who turned suburban mall culture and trance into the sound of the city's underground.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · NOVEMBER 25, 2025 · 3 MIN READ

Tohji is the hardest rapper in Tokyo to have a normal conversation with. When Dazed interviewed him in 2022, he kept his camera off, answered through his girlfriend in Japanese, let his manager do most of the talking, and once left the Zoom call entirely, returning ten minutes later with no explanation. The interviewer called it an acutely bizarre exchange. It doesn't matter. Tohji's music says everything his press shyness won't — and it says it at maximum volume.

One half of Mall Boyz alongside his friend and collaborator gummyboy, Tohji is the figurehead of Tokyo's Drain-adjacent underground: a terminally online generation of mall kids making what Dazed called SoundCloud rap fused with euphoric 90s eurotrance and J-pop, engineered for a food court with a world-class soundsystem.

His 2022 album T-Mix was the proof of scale. Eleven tracks, executive-produced by Tokyo producer Banvox, and a guest list like a global underground all-star team: Yeule, Bladee, Mechatok, London's Palmistry. Mechatok produced "WATER WAY," "HANABI," and "UFO"; "Twilight Zone" paired Tohji with Bladee himself. A Tokyo rapper who came up on 2018's scrappy Mall Tape was now trading records with Stockholm and London — without leaving his own frequency.

WHY TOKYO CLAIMS HIM

Tohji is the city dialed to one wavelength: the suburban shopping mall, the train home at midnight, the fluorescent glow of a Shibuya side street at 3am. Mall Boyz's "POOLSIDE" video made that world literal, and the MallTapeRemixZone series — with LEX, kZm, elleteresa, JP THE WAVY orbiting — showed an entire community trading ideas on his turf.

THE KEY TRACK

"ULTRA RARE" (2022). The Mechatok-produced single from T-Mix is Tohji in full flight — trance synths pitched into the stratosphere and a hook built to be screamed by a thousand kids in a mall parking lot.

THE SOUND

Eurotrance arpeggios, J-pop sugar, SoundCloud rap drums — Tohji stacks all three and finds the joy nobody else knew was in there. The Thrilliam Angels-produced broken ep (January 2022) showed the darker end of the spectrum, his feature on Yeule's Glitch Princess ("Perfect Blue") showed he could drift, and his 2020 single "Oreo" — remixed by both Mura Masa and Malibu — proved his hooks travel anywhere.

START HERE

"ULTRA RARE," then "Twilight Zone" for the Bladee link-up, then Mall Tape-era "mallin'" for the raw origin story. Finish with KUUGA, his 2021 collaborative project with fellow MC Loota and French producer Brodinski.

Tohji doesn't pretend to be mysterious — he just doesn't need you to understand him. Listen louder.
