ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · TORONTO

RIVER TIBER

Toronto's ghostwriter-in-plain-sight — his falsetto has been sampled by Drake and Kendrick while his own records stay beautifully under the radar.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · JANUARY 28, 2026 · 3 MIN READ

Listen → Listen on Spotify

Start with “Hypnotized” — the official video for the Dreaming Eyes single Kendrick lifted for “Savior.”

You've heard River Tiber a hundred times and didn't know it. Tommy Paxton-Beesley — Toronto-born, classically trained on cello, Berklee-schooled — is the singer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist whose music keeps showing up on other people's blockbusters. Drake's "No Tellin'" is built on his "No Talk." SZA's "Broken Clocks" has his fingerprints. Kendrick Lamar's "Savior" samples his "Hypnotized." And his own albums — self-released, achingly beautiful alt-R&B — remain the best-kept secret in the city that won't stop sampling him.

The 2016 debut Indigo, self-released after a run of EPs, is the Rosetta Stone: synth-doused, falsetto-led R&B with deep bass and intricate drums, featuring Daniel Caesar and Tess Parks, with "Acid Test" shortlisted for the SOCAN Songwriting Prize and winning a MuchMusic Video Award. "Illusions" — a single with Pusha T and Kaytranada — hinted at the scale he could operate on, but Paxton-Beesley kept his own name small and his pen everywhere else. The 2022 follow-up Dreaming Eyes leaned further into psychedelic, jazz-tinged R&B, and the yearly EPs keep coming — a quiet, steady output from a guy who could credibly claim co-authorship of the last decade of hip-hop.

WHY TORONTO CLAIMS HIM

He's the connective tissue of the city's whole scene. Returned to Toronto after Berklee, wrote for the underground, got looped into the OVO gravity — and instead of chasing the coattails, he built his own catalog and kept collaborating on his terms, from Kaytranada's Polaris-winning 99.9% to BADBADNOTGOOD's Sour Soul to OVO signee Smiley's "Spill the Tea." The stars get the stadiums; River Tiber gets the "who did this?" credits.

THE KEY TRACK

"Hypnotized" — the Dreaming Eyes single that Kendrick lifted for "Savior." Hearing it on its own is the revelation: a slow-burning, hypnotic R&B cut that sounds like it was always meant to be both a secret and a hit. Play it next to Kendrick's version and marvel at how much of the song's soul survived the trip.

THE VOICE

The falsetto is the instrument — floating, Thom Yorke-adjacent, layered over his own multi-instrumental arrangements. He's a cellist playing producer, and it shows: everything is arranged, everything breathes, nothing is wasted.

START HERE

"Hypnotized," then Indigo front to back, then Dreaming Eyes. Chase the writing credits after — "Broken Clocks," "AstroThunder," "I Keep Calling" — and try not to get dizzy.

River Tiber is Toronto's most sampled secret. The city knows. Now you do too.
