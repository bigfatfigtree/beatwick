ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · TORONTO

LOONY

Scarborough's neo-soul secret — two Polaris longlists, a SOCAN nomination, Elton John's obsession, and a Metro Boomin sample. Still independent.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · FEBRUARY 18, 2026 · 3 MIN READ

Listen → Listen on Spotify

Start with “raw” — the soft thing live session — jazzy, vulnerable, unfiltered.

Toronto's R&B conversation usually starts and ends with two names. LOONY is the best argument for looking past them. Kira Huszar, from Scarborough, makes neo-soul that sounds like the genre's golden era rebuilt for right now — smoky, playful, spiky, and stacked with the kind of vocal performances that make other singers nervous. Her 2020 EP JOYRiDE and 2021's soft thing both earned Polaris Music Prize longlist spots; her 2023 self-titled album made it three. Elton John played her on his Rocket Hour and declared her one of his favorite artists coming out of Canada. She's doing all of this without a major-label machine behind her.

JOYRiDE announced her as a fully-formed weirdo: "WHiTE LiE" pairs creamy synths and funky bass with a tongue like a knife, and the EP moves from gospel-tinged joy to brooding electronic soul without breaking stride. soft thing — recorded with collaborators including Akeel Henry, once an apprentice to Noah "40" Shebib — is the deeper record, a lockdown-era study of vulnerability with production from Adam Pondang and Jordon Manswell that draws fair Erykah Badu and Sade comparisons. "raw," its most exposed moment, got a second life in 2024 when Metro Boomin sampled it for 21 Savage and Burna Boy's "just like me." Scarborough's underground went global through a two-minute soul song.

WHY TORONTO CLAIMS HER

She's a product of the city's deepest bench. Toured with Rhye, worked with the orbit around BADBADNOTGOOD, got the Toronto Star treatment before most people knew her name. And she wears Scarborough on her sleeve — the Bluffs, the housing estates, the contradiction between them — in a way that makes her songs feel like neighborhood journalism. The 2020 SOCAN Songwriting Prize nomination for "Some Kinda Love" just confirmed the pen is real.

THE KEY TRACK

"raw." Vulnerable, jazzy, built on knocking drums and horns, with a vocal that shifts from playful staccato to honeyed melody in the same breath. It's the song that convinced Elton John and Metro Boomin in the same career — two very different rooms, same verdict.

THE PERSONA

Defiant, funny, and unwilling to be sanded down. LOONY's lyrics are romantic without being soft and sharp without being cruel — a conversational wit that makes the love songs feel like late-night arguments you actually want to have. Solange comparisons followed her early; she outgrew them by simply being more herself.

START HERE

soft thing first, then JOYRiDE for the playfulness, then the 2023 self-titled LOONY for the fully-assembled version. "Some Kinda Love" for the pen.

LOONY is Toronto's best-kept R&B secret hiding in plain sight. The longlists keep coming. The labels can wait.
