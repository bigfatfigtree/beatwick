ARTIST SPOTLIGHT · LOS ANGELES

PINK SIIFU

The most prolific shape-shifter in underground rap — from Birmingham to Los Angeles, his GUMBO'! folds jazz, punk, and Southern rap into one simmering pot.

BY THE BEATWICK DESK · DECEMBER 17, 2025 · 3 MIN READ

Listen → Listen on Spotify

Start with “Gumbo'! 4 tha Folks, Hold On” — the GUMBO'! opener — family reunion and all.

Some artists release albums. Pink Siifu releases weather systems. Born Livingston Matthews, split between Birmingham and Cincinnati before relocating to Los Angeles in 2013, Siifu has spent the last decade putting out dozens of projects — EPs, albums, mixtapes, collaborations — at a pace that makes prolific look like a hobby. One record is hazy jazz-rap, the next is abrasive punk-rap, the next is ambient soul, and all of them sound like him. He's the underground's ultimate shapeshifter, and LA is where he built the laboratory.

The centerpiece is 2021's GUMBO'! — a title that's less an album name than a thesis. Across the record, Siifu folds Southern rap, neo-soul, jazz, and punk into a simmering whole, with Georgia Anne Muldrow and Nick Hakim on vocals and production from The Alchemist and Monte Booker. It landed exactly where his best work does: too adventurous for the mainstream, too undeniable for the underground to ignore. Earlier, 2018's Ensley — named for the Birmingham neighborhood where his grandmother lived — made Pitchfork's best rap albums list with nothing but hushed whispers and ambient jazz tapestries. Then 2020's NEGRO detonated that quiet entirely, all distortion and rage. Same artist, different dimensions.

WHY LOS ANGELES CLAIMS HIM

Birmingham made him, Cincinnati raised him, but Los Angeles is where Pink Siifu became Pink Siifu — the city where he recorded with Syd, linked with the beat scene's orbit, and found the collaborators who could match his restlessness. The LA underground runs on artists who refuse a lane, and nobody refuses harder: he produces as iiye, formed the FlySiifu's duo with Fly Anakin for a Wu-Tang-sharp 2020 tape, and keeps one foot in the Leimert-adjacent jazz world his father, a saxophonist, wired into him from birth.

THE KEY TRACK

"Gumbo'! 4 tha Folks, Hold On" — the opening suite of GUMBO'!, featuring Big Rube, Liv.e, Nick Hakim, and DJ Harrison. It stacks voices like a family reunion, prayer and weed smoke in equal measure, and it contains the whole Siifu proposition: earnest, experimental, and too shy to be self-serious about any of it.

THE WORK RATE

46-plus EPs, albums, and mixtapes in the 2010s alone, and the 2020s haven't slowed — Real Bad Flights (2022), IT'S TOO QUIET..'!! (2023), BLACK'!ANTIQUE (2025). Nobody else in rap has a catalog this deep this fast. Keeping up is a full-time job; the reward is never being bored.

START HERE

GUMBO'! first, then Ensley for the quiet version, then NEGRO for the loud one. FlySiifu's with Fly Anakin is the fun detour.

Pink Siifu doesn't do eras. He does everything, all the time, from Los Angeles.
