The edge is local.

Genre is dead. Nobody wakes up a fan of "electronic music" — they wake up a fan of Berlin, of Lagos, of whatever is shaking the walls three streets over. Music doesn't come from genres. It comes from places.

The big music press can't do cities. They parachute in, file 800 words, and leave. We do the opposite: 30 cities, each with its own page, its own auto-updating playlist, its own venues, its own dispatches. The scenes, the rooms, the tracks actually moving — refreshed weekly, written with teeth.

We don't do dry recaps. We don't do press-release journalism. Every dispatch has a take: why this city sounds like this, who's moving it, and what the hype machine gets wrong.

Start anywhere. Every city has a sound. Find yours.
