What is Beatwick? A music-discovery platform organized by city — 30 markets from New York to Lagos to Tokyo. Each city gets its own radio-station page: trending tracks, artist spotlights, scene dispatches, venue guides and an auto-updating Spotify playlist. We cover the scenes the algorithm misses, in a voice that doesn't flatter.
